Autonomous Sales Recovery Agent

Identify quotations that have not become orders and give sales teams a repeatable follow-up process.

Prioritized follow-up · production revenue figures not disclosed

The storyArchitectureTechnical decisions

Sales teams send quotations that do not always become orders. Inconsistent or delayed follow-up can leave those opportunities unattended.

I built an agent that monitors quotations that have not become sales orders, prioritizes follow-up using value and age, and triggers follow-up actions through business workflows.

Turns follow-up decisions into a repeatable workflow. Exact production revenue figures are confidential.

Technical decisions & boundaries

  1. Opportunity detection

    The workflow compares quotations with sales-order information to identify opportunities that still need follow-up.

  2. Decision logic

    Quotation value and age support prioritization. Business rules and lead-time analysis keep follow-up timing tied to the sales process.

  3. Action handoff

    Power Automate connects decisions to email, tasks, and notifications. This public diagram does not claim a particular customer-send approval policy.

  4. Outcome boundary

    The project makes follow-up repeatable. Exact recovered revenue and production conversion figures remain confidential.
