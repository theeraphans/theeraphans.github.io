Visual Product Recognition & Shelf Analytics

Convert shelf photos into product and availability data, reducing the work needed for a store audit.

45 → 15 minutes per store · 750+ SKU classes

The storyArchitectureTechnical decisions

Turning a shelf photo into product and availability data.

The problem

Manual store audits took time and were difficult to scale across product categories and changing shelf conditions.

My contribution

I developed and deployed detection, classification, and similarity search, built Flask APIs and OpenCV post-processing, and connected the outputs to downstream BI analysis.

What changed

Audit time fell from 45 to 15 minutes per store. The system covered 750+ SKU classes and achieved 90% recognition accuracy.

Technical decisions & boundaries

  1. Detection first

    Detection locates products before recognition. This separates the task of finding an item from deciding which SKU it is.

  2. Recognition evidence

    Classification and similarity search contribute to product recognition. The public description does not specify an unpublished fusion rule or model threshold.

  3. Serving and post-processing

    Flask APIs expose the pipeline. OpenCV post-processing turns predictions into outputs that downstream analysis can use.

  4. Measured result

    Audit time fell from 45 to 15 minutes, about 67% less time. The reported recognition accuracy was 90%; detailed evaluation conditions are not public.
