01 / INDEPENDENT PROJECT

Agentic AI Platform

A reusable foundation for agents that need to do more than answer a question.

The problem

Each workflow needs routing, context, tools, and controls for consequential actions. Rebuilding these capabilities for every agent makes evaluation and maintenance harder.

What I designed

I built a local toolkit for defining agents and their tasks, connecting them to business tools, and checking their answers against example cases. A separate review process lets a person inspect suggested changes before promoting an agent version.

Technical design & architecture

A FastAPI core coordinates execution and state. MCP provides the business-tool interface. Evaluation and execution traces support failure diagnosis and controlled version promotion.

Current stage: local implementation with services for agent execution, chat channels, business tools, and evaluation, alongside test suites. I inspected the source for this update; this page does not claim a production deployment or a completed live end-to-end validation.

02 / EMPLOYER WORK

Digital Transformation

Customer intelligence, sales recovery, and the data foundation behind them.

These initiatives connect operational workflows with AI and reusable enterprise data.

VOC Inquiry Intelligence

Manual analysis of more than 3,000 emails and 600 calls a day was slow and inconsistent.

I built a pipeline that prepares emails, transcribes calls, and identifies the type of inquiry and customer feedback. A web portal lets people review the results before the reviewed data goes to CRM and Power BI.

The workflow covers 108 inquiry types and supports urgent-case review, customer satisfaction analysis, and reporting. Exact production accuracy and response-time figures are confidential.

Technical design & architecture

Azure OpenAI handles transcription and classification. LlamaIndex and Milvus retrieve inquiry definitions. LangChain, CrewAI, and LangFuse support orchestration and tracing. People review results before CRM and Power BI delivery.

Autonomous Sales Recovery Agent

Sales teams send quotations that do not always become orders. Inconsistent or delayed follow-up can leave those opportunities unattended.

I built an agent that monitors quotations that have not become sales orders, prioritizes follow-up using value and age, and triggers follow-up actions through business workflows.

Turns follow-up decisions into a repeatable workflow. Exact production revenue figures are confidential.

Technical design & architecture

Heuristic inference, business rules, lead-time analysis, and Airflow.

Data Platform / Power BI

Scattered business data made reporting and automation harder to scale.

I built the data foundation with Airflow, dbt, Docker, and SQL, established centralized warehouse models, and enabled downstream Power BI and automation.

Centralizes reporting logic into reusable models for analytics and automation. Exact adoption figures are confidential.

03 / EMPLOYER WORK

Visual Product Recognition & Shelf Analytics

Turning a shelf photo into product and availability data.

The problem

Manual store audits took time and were difficult to scale across product categories and changing shelf conditions.

What I built

I developed and deployed detection, classification, and similarity search, built Flask APIs and OpenCV post-processing, and connected the outputs to downstream BI analysis.

What changed

Audit time fell from 45 to 15 minutes per store. The system covered 750+ SKU classes and achieved 90% recognition accuracy.

Technical design & architecture

YOLO, CNN / ViT, FAISS, KNN, OpenCV, Flask, and Docker.

04 / INDEPENDENT PROJECT · IN DEVELOPMENT

Hermes Enterprise AI Workspace

An enterprise workspace around an autonomous agent.

The problem

Teams need agent capabilities without exposing credentials or losing control of conversation ownership, tool activity, and cost.

What I'm building

I am building a workspace where people can define an agent, check its configuration, publish a version, and choose which agents work together. The workspace also provides conversations, task tracking, approval requests, and controls for document knowledge and memory.

Technical design & boundaries

The FastAPI BFF enforces identity and session ownership, keeps the Hermes API credential out of the browser, and proxies streaming execution. Honcho provides persistent memory context. A configurable Cognee knowledge service supports document retrieval, with a user-scoped local index as a fallback.

Agent building and runtime execution are separate paths. Published factory versions become available for runtime-team selection; publishing does not automatically start a conversation.

Current stage: a local portal and backend prototype. Enterprise SSO, RBAC, multi-user reliability testing, and deployment documentation remain planned. Local setup documentation and test suites exist; live integration and multi-user production reliability are not claimed here. A public repository and recorded demo are not released.
